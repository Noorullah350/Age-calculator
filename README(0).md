# Age Calculator

A modern, offline-ready age calculator that shows your exact age live — down to the second.

Enter your date of birth (and optionally your birth time), and the calculator instantly shows your age broken down into years, months, weeks, days, hours, minutes, and seconds — updating live in real time.

## Features

- **Live counters** — minutes and seconds tick upward in real time, no refresh needed
- **Optional birth time** — for a more precise, second-accurate result
- **Detailed breakdown** — years, months, days, weeks, hours, minutes, seconds, quarters, and day of the week you were born
- **Next birthday countdown** — see exactly how many days are left
- **5 color themes** — Sand, Rose, Mint, Violet, and Sky
- **Fully responsive** — works smoothly on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- **100% offline** — no internet connection, external fonts, or dependencies required. Just open the file in any browser.

## How to use

1. Download `age-calculator.html` from this repository
2. Open it in any web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
3. Enter your date of birth
4. (Optional) Enter your birth time for a precise result
5. Click **Calculate age**

No installation, no internet, no build steps — it just works.
